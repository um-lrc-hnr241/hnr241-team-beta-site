Plot Rules

A few simple guidelines help our garden stay healthy and welcoming for everyone.

  • Plots must be planted and maintained by May 15 each year.
  • No pesticides — organic methods only.
  • Please keep pathways clear for other members.
  • Annual plot fee: $25, due each March.

Questions?

Email greenwoodgarden@example.org and we'll be happy to help.
